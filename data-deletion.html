Data Deletion Request

መፅሐፍ ቅዱስ (Ethiopian Orthodox Bible)

How to Delete Your Data

All data in this app is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store any data on external servers. Therefore, you have two options to delete your data:

Option 1: Clear App Data (Recommended)

  1. Open your Android device Settings
  2. Go to Apps or Application Manager
  3. Find and tap on መፅሐፍ ቅዱስ (Ethiopian Orthodox Bible)
  4. Tap Storage
  5. Tap Clear Data (or "Clear Storage")
  6. Confirm the action

This will permanently delete all your:

  • Bookmarks
  • Highlights
  • Notes
  • Reading progress
  • Theme and language preferences

Option 2: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling the app will also permanently delete all locally stored data.

Data Types Stored by the App

  • Bookmarks - Your saved verses
  • Highlights - Colored verse highlights
  • Notes - Your personal notes
  • Reading Progress - History of chapters read
  • App Preferences - Theme (dark/light) and language settings

Important: This app does not collect personal information. There is no account, no email, no location data, and no data sent to external servers. All data deletion is performed locally on your device.
