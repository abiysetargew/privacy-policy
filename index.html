Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

መፅሐፍ ቅዱስ (Holy Bible) is a free Bible application for Ethiopian Orthodox Christians. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone)
  • No location data
  • No contact information
  • No financial information
  • No browsing history

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored ONLY on your device and never transmitted to external servers:

  • Bookmarks - Verses you save
  • Highlights - Colored highlights on verses
  • Notes - Personal notes you create
  • Reading Progress - Your reading history
  • Theme Preference - Dark/Light mode
  • Language Preference - Selected Bible language
  • Reading Plans - Your reading plan progress

All local data can be deleted by clearing the app's data in your Android settings.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party libraries:

  • share_plus - Allows you to share Bible verses via other apps that you choose (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.). Only invoked when you tap the share button.

None of these services collect personal information from your use of this app.

Offline Operation

The Bible text, fonts, and all app content are bundled with the application. The app is designed to operate fully offline and does not require an internet connection for core functionality. The app does not request any internet permission.

Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from children or adults. It is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
